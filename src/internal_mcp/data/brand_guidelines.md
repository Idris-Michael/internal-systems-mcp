# Ledgerly brand guidelines (fictional practice brand)

Ledgerly is a made-up accounting app for small businesses. Nothing here is real Intuit material.

## Voice
- Plain, warm, confident. Speak to a busy shop owner, not an accountant.
- Short sentences. One idea per line.
- Never promise results we cannot guarantee ("double your profit").

## Hard rules (checked in code by qa_check_ad)
- Colours: only the palette in templates.json > palette.
- CTA: only from the approved list.
- Every ad carries the legal line exactly as written.
- Banned words: guarantee, guaranteed, free forever, instant, risk-free.
- Headline and body must fit the character limits of the template slot.

## Soft guidance (for the prompt, judged by a person or a model)
- Lead with the customer's outcome, then the feature.
- Do: "Get paid faster. Send invoices in two taps."
- Don't: "Revolutionary AI-powered invoicing synergy."
